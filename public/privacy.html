MindForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

MindForge is an educational puzzle and trivia app. We do not require an account and do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or location.

Local storage

Puzzle progress, settings, and optional question reports are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you manually export it.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting in the current version.

Children

MindForge is intended for general audiences. It is not directed exclusively at children under 13.
